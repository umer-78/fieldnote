Fieldnote

Scanned paperwork is searchable only once it has text. Fieldnote reads each page with OCR once, when it is added, keeps every line with its position, and answers questions like “the total on the Popular Book receipt from 5 March” by finding the page, reading the value, and showing the crop of the page it came from, so a person can check it at a glance.
